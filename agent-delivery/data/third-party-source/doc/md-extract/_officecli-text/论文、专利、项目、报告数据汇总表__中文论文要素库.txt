China’s Civil Code does not copy this formulation directly; instead, it generally stipulates the general personality right arising from personal freedom and human dignity.
The provision on general personality right is a framework provision, and general personality right is a legal interest protected by civil law, encompassing two core values: human dignity and personal freedom. Specifically, human dignity refers, on the one hand, to the subject’s entitlement to legal protection of its capacity for civil rights, and on the other hand, to the abstract totality of personality interests protected by law by virtue of such capacity. Personal freedom refers to all personal freedoms related to the realization of human dignity in the entire social life of every individual.
